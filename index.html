Accountability & Governance — Ghana

Public money, made legible.

Government audit and oversight reports are long, dense, and rarely read outside a small circle. The Audit Ledger turns them into clear visual data — what was found, how much money is involved, and what happened next. No sign-up required.

About this project

A sibling project to Labari Data

Each report page is built from the official published document from the relevant institution (currently the Ghana Audit Service). Figures are transcribed and checked against the source PDF; where a breakdown hasn't been transcribed yet, it's marked as a placeholder rather than guessed at.

Data sourced from the Ghana Audit Service (audit.gov.gh) and public reporting.
