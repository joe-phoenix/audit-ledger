2023 Annual Report on Ministries, Departments and Other Agencies

The Auditor-General's report covering the public accounts of ministries, departments and agencies for the year ended 31 December 2023. Irregularities are grouped into seven categories.
Source: Ghana Audit Service, full report (PDF) Publisher: Ghana Audit Service
